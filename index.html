Welcome, Astronomer! 🔭

  1. 👉 Aim — click a planet, galaxy or star in the list, or click the Sky Map.
  2. 🌍 Follow — the Earth spins, so the sky slowly drifts away. Follow keeps up with it.
  3. 🎯 Guiding — locks onto a star to stop the telescope wobbling.
  4. ✨ AO — the air above us is bumpy and makes things blurry and shimmery. Adaptive optics bends a special mirror hundreds of times a second to undo it!
  5. 📸 Take Picture — hold still while the camera collects light.
  6. 💾 Save — put your picture in the Gallery!

Moving around: arrow keys (or the arrow buttons). Tap for a tiny nudge, hold to go faster.

Shortcuts: Space = Follow, G = Guiding, O = AO, P = Take Picture.

Collect them all — and see if you can track down the mystery object! 🕵️
